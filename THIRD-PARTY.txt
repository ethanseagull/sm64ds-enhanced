Super Mario 64 DS Enhanced builder: what's in it

None of Super Mario 64 DS is in this program. Everything of the game in the
.cia it makes comes from the dump you give it, on your PC, so the .cia is for
your own consoles. Please don't share it.

Super Mario 64 DS is (c) Nintendo. This is a fan project and isn't affiliated
with Nintendo.

- game3ds.elf: the 3DS port, built on tangosdev's Super Mario 64 DS
  decompilation and PC port (MIT, below). It includes libctru and citro3d
  (devkitPro, zlib, below) and SIMDe (MIT, below).
- The builder's extractor (Extract.cs) is the PC port's extract_assets.ps1
  in C# (same project and licence).
- makerom.exe 0.19.0, from Project_CTR: https://github.com/3DSGuy/Project_CTR
  (MIT, below).
- bannertool.exe 1.2.3: https://github.com/carstene1ns/3ds-bannertool
  (MIT, below).
- banner.wav (the HOME Menu sound) and music.mp3 (the builder's music, a
  lullaby remake): made by ethan.
