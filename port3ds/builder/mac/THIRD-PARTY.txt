Super Mario 64 DS Enhanced builder for macOS: what's in it

None of Super Mario 64 DS is in this app. Everything of the game in the .cia
it makes comes from the dump you give it, on your Mac, so the .cia is for your
own consoles. Please don't share it.

Super Mario 64 DS is (c) Nintendo. This is a fan project and isn't affiliated
with Nintendo.

- MacOS/SM64DSEnhancedBuilder: the builder, a Swift port of the Windows
  builder and of the PC port's extractor, extract_assets.ps1 (tangosdev's
  Super Mario 64 DS decompilation and PC port, MIT, below).
- Resources/payload/game3ds.elf: the 3DS port, built on tangosdev's Super
  Mario 64 DS decompilation and PC port (MIT, below). It includes libctru and
  citro3d (devkitPro, zlib, below) and SIMDe (MIT, below).
- Helpers/makerom 0.19.0, from Project_CTR (MIT, below), built for macOS from
  https://github.com/3DSGuy/Project_CTR/tree/e8f5f529c54ff9b22a2491a480ffa69206bf7b19
  It includes mbedtls (Apache 2.0, below), libyaml (MIT, below) and CUE's blz
  (GPL 3 or later, below; its source is makerom/deps/libblz at that link).
- Helpers/bannertool 1.2.3 (MIT, below), built for macOS from
  https://github.com/carstene1ns/3ds-bannertool/tree/da573b13418acd0ac1803b43b30e8a155e5f83e2
  It includes stb_image and stb_vorbis (Sean Barrett) and dr_wav (David
  Reid), which are public domain.
- banner.wav (the HOME Menu sound) and music.mp3 (the builder's music, a
  lullaby remake): made by ethan.
